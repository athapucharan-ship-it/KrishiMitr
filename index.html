A few things to know before you get growing.

How do I know which soil I have?

Look at your soil's texture, drainage, and how it feels when moist. The photos here are illustrative, not a soil-identification test. For a reliable answer, ask a local agricultural officer or have a sample tested for texture, pH, and nutrients.

Will every listed crop grow in my field?

The lists are starting points, not guaranteed planting recommendations. Rainfall, irrigation, climate, drainage, soil pH, and local varieties all matter. Check local recommendations and a recent soil test before choosing a crop.

Can I watch the guides in Telugu?

Yes. Disease guides include topic-matched Telugu YouTube resources where a source is available. For other diseases, use the crop-specific Telugu YouTube search. Third-party videos are educational and are not a substitute for diagnosis or locally approved advice.

Where are my favorite crops saved?

Your favorites and theme are linked to your KrishMithr account and are available after you sign in on another device.
